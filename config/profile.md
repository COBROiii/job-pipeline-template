# Standard form fields

Used to fill the routine parts of application forms. Fill in every TODO before first use. This file holds personal data, so keep your repo private.

**Every personal fact a form asks for lives here.** Fill forms from this file. If a form asks something not covered and the answer is obvious and routine (title, mapping a predicted grade to a dropdown, an integrated Masters counting as a Masters, office preferences), Claude picks it, carries on and lists its assumptions for you afterwards. Anything else (eligibility, diversity, declarations, salary, your experience) it asks you first, then adds your answer here (with the date).
